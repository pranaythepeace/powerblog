# **📄 Process Custom Documents with AI Builder**  
📅 **Published Date:** March 5, 2025 &nbsp;&nbsp; ✍ **Author:** Pranay Reddy Muthyala  

## **📌 Introduction**  
Processing custom documents manually can be **time-consuming and prone to errors**. Microsoft's **AI Builder** offers a **Document Processing feature** that automates **data extraction** from various document types, enhancing both **efficiency and accuracy**.  

In this blog, we will explore how to **create a document processing model in AI Builder** and integrate it into Power Automate and Power Apps to improve workflow automation.  

## **🧠 Understanding Document Processing with AI Builder**  
AI Builder's **Document Processing** allows users to extract **specific information** from **structured, semi-structured, or unstructured documents**. By training models on sample documents, AI Builder learns to identify and extract relevant data fields, streamlining workflows and reducing manual intervention.  

## **🛠️ Creating Your First Document Processing Model**  
To harness the power of AI Builder for document processing, follow these steps:  

1️⃣ **Access AI Builder** – Sign in to **Power Automate** and navigate to the **AI Hub**.  

![AI Model](/images/ProcesscustomdocumentswithAIBuilder/AI-Models.webp)

![Model AI Extract information](/images/ProcesscustomdocumentswithAIBuilder/Extract-information-from-invoices.webp)

2️⃣ **Select Document Type** – Choose between **structured, semi-structured, or unstructured documents** based on your needs.  

![Choose Document Type](/images/ProcesscustomdocumentswithAIBuilder/Choose-document-type.webp)

3️⃣ **Define Data Fields** – Specify the **fields and tables** you want to extract, such as **invoice numbers, total amounts, or customer details**.  

![Choose Information To Extract](/images/ProcesscustomdocumentswithAIBuilder/Choose-information-to-extract.webp)

4️⃣ **Upload Sample Documents** – Provide at least **five sample documents per layout** to train the model effectively.  

![Add Collection of Documents](/images/ProcesscustomdocumentswithAIBuilder/Add-collections-of-documents.webp)

![Review Documents](/images/ProcesscustomdocumentswithAIBuilder/Review-documents.webp)

![Tag Documents](/images/ProcesscustomdocumentswithAIBuilder/Tag-documents.webp)

5️⃣ **Train and Publish** – After configuring, **train the model and publish it** for use in applications or workflows.  

![Train Your Model](/images/ProcesscustomdocumentswithAIBuilder/Train-your-model.webp)

![Model Training](/images/ProcesscustomdocumentswithAIBuilder/Model-training.webp)

![Test Model](/images/ProcesscustomdocumentswithAIBuilder/Test-model.webp)

![Model Test Review](/images/ProcesscustomdocumentswithAIBuilder/model-test-review.webp)

## **🔄 Integrating the Model into Your Workflow**  
Once your model is trained and published, you can **integrate it into business processes** through **Power Automate** and **Power Apps**.  

![use Of the Model](/images/ProcesscustomdocumentswithAIBuilder/use-of-the-model.webp)

### **📌 Power Automate**  
- Create **flows** that automatically process incoming documents.  
- Extract key data and **perform actions like database updates, notifications, or approvals**.  

### **📌 Power Apps**  
- Develop applications that **utilize the model to analyze documents in real-time**.  
- Provide **immediate insights and data extraction** for users.  

## **📊 Benefits of Using AI Builder for Document Processing**  
✔ **Efficiency** – Automates repetitive tasks, allowing employees to focus on strategic activities.  
✔ **Accuracy** – Reduces human errors associated with manual data entry.  
✔ **Scalability** – Easily handles large volumes of documents, adapting to growing business needs.  

By leveraging **AI Builder’s Document Processing capabilities**, organizations can transform their **document management workflows**, leading to **increased productivity and operational excellence**. 🚀
